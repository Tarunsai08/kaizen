Kaizen — Privacy

Kaizen is a personal app. It has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you enter stays in a database on your phone.

Health data

With your permission, Kaizen reads steps and sleep sessions from Health Connect to show daily steps, fill in your sleep log, calculate sleep debt and auto-complete step habits. Kaizen never writes to Health Connect and never sends this data anywhere.

Screen time & Shield

Usage access is used only to show time spent per app on this phone. The optional Shield (Accessibility) only checks which app and screen is open, to leave Shorts/Reels feeds and to show a pause before apps you choose. Nothing is recorded except simple counters.

Your control

You can export or erase all data in Settings at any time, and revoke permissions in Android settings.
